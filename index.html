How it starts

Three steps, and the first one is the only form you will ever fill in.

  1. Register

    Your name, your WhatsApp number, and what you do. That last part matters: everything it suggests later fits your kind of work.

  2. Your assistant texts you first

    Connect Gmail, Outlook or your calendar from a link in the chat. Sign-in goes through Google or Microsoft, so Assistyca never sees your password.

  3. Just text

    It answers from your real mail and calendar, sets things up when you ask, and checks with you before anything it cannot undo.

What it works from

Connect what you use. Skip what you don't. Anything it does once, it can do on a schedule.

  • Your inbox

    Gmail and Outlook. What arrived, what matters, what someone never sent, and a long thread in three lines.

  • Your calendar

    What is on today, where the gaps are, and an hour that works for two people.

  • Receipts and folders

    It chases the missing ones, gathers a month into one folder, and answers what you spent and with whom.

  • Lists and to-dos

    Shopping, packing, the jobs for the week. Give an item a due date and it nudges you each morning about what is due or late.

  • Reminders

    Once, at a time you name, or every week. Text me at 7 with what is on. Every Sunday, remind me to call mum.

  • Watching the web

    A competitor's prices, a venue's availability, tickets going on sale. It looks on a schedule and messages you when something changes.
